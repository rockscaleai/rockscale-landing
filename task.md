Copywriting Guidelines

Write concise, confident and professional copy.

Avoid clichés.

Avoid generic phrases like:

“We’d love to hear from you.”

Instead focus on business value.

Examples:

Tell us about your AI initiative.

Discuss your digital transformation goals.

Let’s explore how AI can create measurable business value.

Speak to executives, founders and technology leaders.
